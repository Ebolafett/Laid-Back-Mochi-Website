ABOUT

One company.

Laid-back Mochi builds and operates a growing portfolio of ventures across commerce, technology, fashion, entertainment, and social impact. We develop companies from the ground up, support promising ideas, and create the foundations for ventures designed to grow beyond their beginnings.
